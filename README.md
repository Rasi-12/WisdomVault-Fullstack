# WisdomVault – AI Elder Knowledge System

**Preserving traditional knowledge. Connecting generations.**

## Overview

WisdomVault is an AI-powered platform designed to preserve traditional knowledge and life experiences shared by elderly people and local communities in different languages and dialects.

Traditional knowledge about herbal medicine, farming practices, weather prediction, food preservation, cultural stories and local traditions is often passed down orally. As communication between generations decreases, valuable knowledge is at risk of being lost.

WisdomVault aims to bridge this gap by using technology to capture, organize, preserve and make this knowledge accessible through web and mobile platforms.

## The Problem

- Valuable traditional knowledge is often shared orally and remains undocumented.
- Local languages and rural dialects create challenges for digital preservation.
- Younger generations have limited access to authentic traditional practices.
- Conventional digital archives lack interactive and intelligent knowledge retrieval.

## Our Solution

We proposed WisdomVault as a hybrid AI-based system that combines human knowledge with artificial intelligence.

The platform focuses on:
- Preserving elders' voices, stories and traditional practices.
- Supporting multilingual and dialect-based knowledge preservation.
- Converting spoken knowledge into structured information.
- Organizing and retrieving preserved knowledge.
- Providing access through web and mobile applications.

## Key Features and Objectives

- Traditional knowledge preservation through voice and text.
- Multilingual and local dialect support.
- Speech-to-text processing.
- Knowledge categorization and organization.
- Semantic search and knowledge retrieval.
- Web and mobile accessibility.
- Community participation and knowledge validation.

## System Methodology

The proposed workflow involves:

1. **Knowledge Collection:** Collecting audio recordings and text-based information from elderly people and local communities.
2. **Speech Processing:** Using speech recognition to convert spoken knowledge into text.
3. **Information Processing:** Applying NLP techniques to identify keywords, languages and relevant entities.
4. **Knowledge Organization:** Categorizing information into domains such as medicine, farming and culture.
5. **Knowledge Retrieval:** Exploring semantic search, knowledge graphs and vector-based retrieval.
6. **User Access:** Providing access to preserved knowledge through web and mobile applications.

## Project Development

The idea behind WisdomVault was initiated to address the loss of traditional knowledge and the need for accessible digital preservation.

Our team collaboratively worked on developing the web platform and mobile application, dividing responsibilities across different development areas.

One of the major challenges was handling local languages and dialects, as preserving and processing regional speech is not straightforward.
